Fried Rice

Description

Rice that is fried

Ingredients

  • A cup of day old rice
  • Your measurement of oil
  • 1 Egg
  • You feel the measurement of Salt
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • Green onions
  • You measure the msg
  • 1 tablespoon of soy sauce

Steps

  1. Firstly prepare all the ingredients
  2. Chop the garlic into small pieces
  3. Next heat the oil in the pan
  4. Then add first the garlic and make it golden
  5. Scramble the egg then cook it make sure to season the egg
  6. Then add the rice into the pan and mix with the egg
  7. Add the salt, msg, and soysauce
  8. Mix them all in the pan
  9. After cooking place it in a plate
  10. Then add green onion to varnish

Thank you! and Enjoy!
