Milo in a Cup

Description

A nice hot cup of milo to enjoy the day!!

Ingredients

  • Hot water
  • Milo
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. Boil water in a kettle
  2. Wait for it to boil
  3. Find a cup to put in the milo
  4. Put in the sugar as well
  5. Pour the hot water inside
  6. Mix well
  7. Then put ice if you want
  8. Pick your milo hot or cold and Enjoy!

Thank you! and Have a wonderful day with milo!
