Hotdog with Rice

Description

Rice with yummy hotdog

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Hotdog
  • Ketchup

Steps

  1. Measure 1 cup of rice
  2. Wash that rice
  3. Then put the rice in the rice cooker
  4. Get a pan and hotdog
  5. Get oil then put it in the pan
  6. Cook the hotdog
  7. Now plate the hotdog and rice
  8. Add ketchup at the side for sauce
  9. Enjoy the food!

Thank you! and Enjoy the hotdog meal!
